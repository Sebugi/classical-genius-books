Le frasi celebri di Robinson Crusoe

In inglese, con traduzione italiana.

I was born in the year 1632, in the city of York, of a good family, though not of that country, my father being a foreigner of Bremen, who settled first at Hull.

Nacqui nell’anno 1632, nella città di York, da buona famiglia, anche se non di quel paese, perché mio padre era uno straniero di Brema, stabilitosi prima a Hull.

I was exceedingly surprised with the print of a man’s naked foot on the shore, which was very plain to be seen on the sand.

Rimasi estremamente sorpreso dall’impronta di un piede nudo d’uomo sulla riva, ben visibile sulla sabbia.

I stood like one thunderstruck, or as if I had seen an apparition.

Restai come fulminato, o come se avessi visto un’apparizione.
