Famous quotes from Myricae

Original English text.

Nel campo mezzo grigio e mezzo nero / resta un aratro senza buoi, che pare / dimenticato, tra il vapor leggero.

In the field half grey and half black a plough without oxen remains, which seems forgotten in the light mist.

San Lorenzo, io lo so perchè tanto / di stelle per l’aria tranquilla / arde e cade, perchè sì gran pianto / nel concavo cielo sfavilla.

San Lorenzo, I know why so many stars burn and fall through the quiet air, why such great weeping sparkles in the hollow sky.

Gemmea l’aria, il sole così chiaro / che tu ricerchi gli albicocchi in fiore, / e del prunalbo l’odorino amaro

The air is gem-like, the sun so clear that you look for apricot trees in blossom and feel the bitter scent of the blackthorn in your heart.

Dov’era la luna? chè il cielo / notava in un’alba di perla,

Where was the moon? For the sky floated in a pearl dawn.

E cielo e terra si mostrò qual era:

And sky and earth showed themselves as they were.

Al campo, dove roggio nel filare / qualche pampano brilla, e dalle fratte / sembra la nebbia mattinal fumare,

In the field, where a few vine leaves glow russet on the row, and from the hedges the morning mist seems to smoke, they are ploughing.
