Famous quotes from Orlando Furioso

Original English text.

Le donne, i cavallier, l’arme, gli amori, / le cortesie, l’audaci imprese io canto, / che furo al tempo che passaro i Mori / d’Africa il mare, e in Francia nocquer tanto,

Of ladies, knights, arms, loves, courtesies and bold deeds I sing, which were in the time when the Moors crossed the sea from Africa and did such harm in France.

Chi mette il piè su l’amorosa pania, / cerchi ritrarlo, e non v’inveschi l’ale; / che non è in somma amor, se non insania, / a giudizio de’ savi universale:

Whoever sets foot on the sticky lime of love, let him try to draw it back and not entangle his wings; for love is in sum nothing but madness, in the universal judgment of the wise.

Orlando, che gran tempo inamorato / fu de la bella Angelica, e per lei / in India, in Media, in Tartaria lasciato / avea infiniti et immortal trofei,

Orlando, who for a long time was in love with beautiful Angelica, and for her in India, Media and Tartary had left infinite and immortal trophies.
